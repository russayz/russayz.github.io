About Me

Hi! I’m Russell, a first-year at the University of Pennsylvania studying Mathematical Economics. Originally from Singapore, I used to sail internationally and served as an officer in the Army.

I first learnt about investing and finance from Reddit during the 2021 meme stock mania. While my younger self was initially driven by the idea of turning a quick profit, I kept investing as I found myself enjoying the experience of forming a variant view and being rewarded when it proved right. That feeling, along with the constant feedback loop that equity markets provide on my own judgement, drew me towards investing so strongly that I turned down a full-ride scholarship to become a government economist.

Outside of markets, I love sailing, soccer, hiking, and great food. Thanks for visiting the site!
