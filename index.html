About Me

Hi! I’m Russell, a first-year student at the University of Pennsylvania studying Mathematical Economics. Originally from Singapore, I used to compete internationally in sailing and served as an artillery officer in the Army.

I first learned about investing and finance from Reddit during the 2021 meme stock mania. While my younger self was initially driven by the idea of turning a quick profit, I kept investing because I found myself enjoying the experience of forming a variant view and being rewarded when it proved right. That feeling, along with the constant feedback that equity markets provide on my own judgment, drew me toward investing so strongly that I turned down a full-ride scholarship to become a government economist.

At Penn, my major in Mathematical Economics equips me with the quantitative skills I need to understand economic drivers and break down complex problems into fundamental relationships that can be mathematically modeled. At the same time, my membership in investment clubs such as the Wharton Investment and Trading Group and Violet Quaker helps me develop the practical skills needed to become a better investor, including financial modeling, valuation, competitive analysis, and business quality assessment.

Outside of markets, I love sailing, soccer, hiking, traveling, current affairs, and great food. Weather permitting, you’ll probably find me outside, either sailing, on a run, or training with my Sunday league soccer team. Always happy to chat!
